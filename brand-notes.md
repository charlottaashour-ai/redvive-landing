# Redvive Brand Book — Key Findings

## What Redvive Actually Is
**Red light therapy studio** — NOT an intimate wellness supplement brand.
- Fully autonomous studios (no staff, no appointments)
- Book on phone, walk in, 10 minutes, science-backed
- Under €30 per session / monthly subscription
- Opening in Helsinki (Punavuori first, then Kallio)
- Pre-launch phase — waitlist landing page

## Master Tagline
**"light is the medicine."**

## Brand Manifesto
"redvive is 10 minutes of doing nothing — so your cells can do everything."

## Key Messages
- All audiences: "wellness, simplified."
- Subscription: "your weekly reset."
- Session: "10 minutes. feel it."
- Athlete/recovery: "recover faster. live longer." (Joonas persona)
- Skin/longevity: "glow from within the cell." (Laura persona)
- Science: "660nm. 850nm. proven."
- Finnish origin: "born in finland. built on light."
- Community: "show up for yourself."

## Mission / Vision / Belief
- Mission: make light therapy a weekly habit
- Vision: the world's most trusted light studio
- Belief: light is the medicine (660nm + 850nm, 5,000+ peer-reviewed studies)

## Four Pillars
- AUTONOMOUS: No staff. Book on your phone. Walk in.
- ACCESSIBLE: Under €30. Monthly subscription. No commitment traps.
- SCIENCE-BACKED: 660nm + 850nm. 5,000+ peer-reviewed studies.
- FINNISH-BUILT: Nordic precision. Private rooms. No excess. Just light.

## Tone of Voice
- Calm & confident (not anxious/pushy) — "660nm for skin. 850nm for everything beneath it."
- Direct & clear (not clinical/cold) — "10 minutes. Full body. No downtime."
- Warm & human (not corporate) — "Give your body a moment. It has earned it."
- Understated Finnish (not boastful) — "Backed by 5,000+ peer-reviewed studies."

## Four Customer Personas
1. **Sara A. — The Optimiser** (34, UX Lead, Kallio) — Tracks HRV, reads Huberman. "Show me the science."
2. **Mikael K. — The Recovering Pro** (41, Sales Director, Töölö) — High stress, poor recovery. "Fast, close by, actually works."
3. **Laura L. — The Longevity Seeker** (48, Entrepreneur, Punavuori) — Invests seriously in herself. "The space and ritual matter."
4. **Joonas H. — The Weekend Athlete** (29, Engineer, Kallio) — Runs, CrossFits, knee injury. "Shave a day off recovery time."

## Visual Identity
- Logo: "redvive" lowercase, sans-serif, bold weight, with distinctive dot on the 'v'
- Brand gradient: Oxblood (#7E0306) → Crimson (#C01A07) → Tia Maria/Vermillion (#D53E0F) → Crusta (#FA8743) — left to right
- Background: Soft rose white #FFF9F9 — NEVER full-screen red
- Red (crimson) reserved for CTAs and key actions ONLY — never decorative
- Typography: Bold sans-serif for logo/headlines, serif italic for taglines

## Website Design Principles (from brand book)
- Soft rose white base (#FFF9F9) throughout — never full-screen red
- Gradient used as hero element (right side), left side stays clean/white
- Crimson for CTAs only
- Brand icons: wavelength, radial mark, panel frame icons
- Finnish copy first (hero in Finnish, English available — never mixed)
- Current phase: pre-launch, waitlist only, gradient-only (no photography yet)

## Photography Direction (for future)
- Person inside cabin, back to camera, facing Helsinki at dusk
- Red LED panels, studio corridor, Helsinki Cathedral visible
- Face bathed in deep red-orange light, eyes closed, peaceful
- NEVER: close-up skin texture, motion blur, bright white clinical lighting, smiling stock couples, nudity, gym aesthetics

## Site Structure (max 4 pages)
- Page 1: Home / Hero (waitlist CTA)
- Page 2: The Science (how red light therapy works)
- Page 3: The Experience (what a session feels like, the studio)
- Page 4: FAQ / About
