### Approach 1: Oxblood Manuscript
**Design Movement:** Dark Romantic Editorial — where luxury perfume campaigns meet rare book typography
**Core Principles:**
- Deep oxblood (#7E0306) as a dominant canvas, creating an atmosphere of intimacy and authority
- Extreme typographic contrast: massive display serif headlines against whisper-thin body copy
- Asymmetric editorial composition with text bleeding off-grid
- Deliberate negative space as a luxury signal

**Color Philosophy:** The palette moves from blood-deep authority (#7E0306) through burning desire (#D53E0F, #FA8743) into warm skin (#FCAF67, #FFDECD). Dark sections feel like velvet; light sections breathe like cream linen.

**Layout Paradigm:** Horizontal text-image split with oversized type running vertically on the left column. Content panels alternate between full-bleed dark and cream. No standard grid — sections are composed like editorial spreads.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rule lines (1px, cream on dark / oxblood on light) as section dividers
- Large numerals (01, 02, 03) as section markers in low-opacity
- Serif type set at extreme sizes (100px+ headlines)

**Interaction Philosophy:** Slow, deliberate hover transitions (600ms ease). Cursor changes to a custom crosshair on product elements. Scroll-triggered fade-in reveals.

**Animation:** Text lines slide in from left on scroll. Product imagery fades in with a warm glow overlay. CTA buttons expand width on hover.

**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond (Display, 700) for headlines / DM Sans (300, 400) for body. Letter-spacing: tight on display, wide on labels.

### Approach 2: Warm Lacquer — Chosen Direction
**Design Movement:** Japanese Minimalism meets Parisian Luxury Beauty — the restraint of Aesop with the warmth of a candlelit atelier
**Core Principles:**
- Warmth as the primary emotional register — every surface glows with amber undertones
- Asymmetric two-column compositions that feel composed, not templated
- Typography as sculpture: headlines set in refined italic serif, body in geometric sans
- Sections transition between deep oxblood and warm nude, creating a breathing rhythm

**Color Philosophy:** #FFDECD as the primary breathing canvas — warm, skin-adjacent, intimate. #7E0306 anchors the brand in authority and depth. The middle tones (#FA8743, #FCAF67) appear as warm light — in gradients, glows, and hover states. #D53E0F is reserved for moments of desire: CTAs, active states, and key brand moments.

**Layout Paradigm:** Offset editorial — headlines break the container edge, images are positioned off-center with generous white space on one side. No symmetric columns. Sections use diagonal clip-path transitions for visual flow.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin circular motifs (partial arcs, not full circles) as decorative elements
- Warm gradient halos behind product imagery
- Oversized italic serif pull-quotes breaking grid

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover states feel like warmth — buttons glow from center outward. Images gain a subtle warm overlay on hover. Smooth 400ms transitions throughout.

**Animation:** Sections reveal with a soft upward drift (translateY 24px → 0, opacity 0 → 1). Hero text appears letter by letter on load. Parallax on hero image.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display (italic, 400/700) for headlines / Jost (300, 400, 500) for body and UI. Headlines feel seductive; body feels clean and modern.

### Approach 3: Copper Ritual
**Design Movement:** Contemporary Craft Luxury — where high-end skincare meets art gallery minimalism
**Core Principles:**
- Cream-first palette with copper and oxblood as punctuation
- Generous whitespace with precise typographic rhythm
- Product as art object: large, isolated, beautifully lit
- Ritual as narrative: the page unfolds like a ceremony

**Color Philosophy:** Begin with #FFDECD as a near-white warmth. Introduce #FCAF67 as copper warmth in icons and accents. Reserve #7E0306 for moments of brand authority. The page feels light, airy, and luxurious — dark sections are used sparingly for maximum impact.

**Layout Paradigm:** Vertical editorial scroll with alternating full-width image panels and text-heavy sections. Product imagery is always given 60%+ of viewport width. Text is set in narrow columns with generous margins.

**Signature Elements:**
- Copper-toned thin borders on cards
- Botanical/organic line illustrations as section dividers
- Staggered card layouts with offset positioning

**Interaction Philosophy:** Gentle and considered — like turning pages of a luxury catalog. Hover reveals additional copy. Smooth scroll snapping between major sections.

**Animation:** Fade-in with scale (1.02 → 1.0) for images. Staggered list reveals for benefit pillars. Subtle parallax on background elements.

**Typography System:** Libre Baskerville (regular, italic) for headlines / Nunito Sans (300, 400) for body. Elegant but approachable.

## Selected Direction: **Approach 2 — Warm Lacquer**

The Warm Lacquer direction best embodies the Redvive brand: intimate warmth, editorial authority, and sensual restraint. The palette moves like a flame — deep oxblood at the base, burning through amber, dissolving into warm cream. Typography pairs seductive italic serif with clean geometric sans. Layout is asymmetric and composed, never templated. Every interaction feels like warmth spreading.
